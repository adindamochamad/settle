# Scope and abort rules

## Timeline

| Day | Date | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Wed 10 Sep | A JSONL run containing a partial whose text differs from the final |
| D2 | Thu 11 Sep | Settling-time-vs-`max_delay` chart exists as an image file |
| D3 | Fri 12 Sep | A public URL someone else can open |
| D4 | Sat 13 Sep | **Feature freeze, 23:59.** Bug fixes only after this |
| D5 | Sun 14 Sep | Every asset recorded. No footage left to shoot |
| D6 | Mon 15 – Tue 16 Sep | Submitted, with at least 6 hours of buffer |

## Priority when time runs out

Sacrifice from the bottom:

1. **The video** — the judging surface for the remote track. Without it there is
   no submission at all.
2. **The numbers** — the originality claim. Without them this is ordinary
   middleware.
3. **The sidecar** — can be demonstrated from replayed JSONL if live fails.
4. **The website** — the most cuttable. Three sections is enough.

## Abort rules

Decide fast, do not deliberate.

- **D2 night, no histogram** → drop the sidecar entirely. Ship the instrument
  and the curve. That is still a complete, still original submission.
- **D4 night, sidecar not working** → demo from replayed JSONL rather than live.
  On video the difference is invisible.
- **D5 midday, video not started** → cut the website to three sections that day.
  The video wins.
- **Partials never differ from finals on any clip** → the clips are too clean.
  Re-record with background noise and faster speech. Do not change the project.
