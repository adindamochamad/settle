The gap

Two numbers, and a blind spot
in between them

Every ASR vendor publishes word error rate — was the final text right — and latency — how fast did it arrive.

Neither describes the interval where the text is visible, plausible, and still changing. Nobody publishes that number.

We call it settling time. This deck is the instrument that measures it, and the sidecar that buys it back.

Method

Ten clips. One instrument.
Four settings swept.

One speaker, recorded at real-time pace, streamed to Speechmatics and logged with arrival timestamps — never interpreted at capture time.

Every clip recorded at max_delay ∈ {0.7, 1.0, 2.0, 4.0} — the engine's own control over when a final is emitted.

Every number that follows traces back to a committed run file. Reproduced with one command: make results.

One feed in.
Two streams out.
